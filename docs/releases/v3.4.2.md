## 更新内容

- **额度环显示优化**：改为上方 5 小时、下方每周的分段圆弧，保留各来源配色；中央使用无底板的品牌 Logo，增强小尺寸下的清晰度，DeepSeek 中空部分填白。环线加粗、断口收紧，悬停分别显示对应窗口的剩余比例，DSH 显示实际余额。
- **Antigravity 额度环常驻**：安装、启用并勾选后保持显示。语言服务器不可用时沿用上次成功快照，重启岛屿也能恢复；没有有效快照或窗口已经重置时显示「暂无数据」，避免显示旧百分比。实时刷新仍需要 Antigravity 语言服务器可用。
- **插件市场头像缓存**：作者头像加载、解码后复用，离开再进入市场时不再重复请求；加载失败时显示姓名首字母。

## What's new

- **Clearer quota indicators**: Separate upper 5-hour and lower weekly arcs retain each provider's color. Larger, clearer brand marks replace app tiles, with DeepSeek's interior filled white. Thicker arcs and smaller gaps improve readability. Hover each half for its remaining quota; DSH shows its actual balance.
- **Persistent Antigravity indicator**: The indicator stays visible when installed, enabled and selected. The last successful snapshot survives language-server downtime and Fly Island restarts. Unknown or expired windows show unavailable data instead of old percentages. Fresh readings still require Antigravity's language server.
- **Cached plugin author avatars**: Decoded avatars are reused across market visits without repeated requests. Failed loads show the author's initial.
