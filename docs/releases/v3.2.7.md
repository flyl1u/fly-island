## 更新内容

- **网易云播放时不再卡顿（#93）**：网易云配合进度插件会把几 MB 的专辑封面原图交给系统，小岛以前每秒读一遍、转一遍，网易云和系统媒体服务因此占用大量 CPU。现在封面缩到显示需要的大小再用，读不到封面时逐步放慢重试，媒体事件也做了节流。
- **开机更轻**：判断网易云版本只读文件头，不再读取整个程序文件；网易云插件的校验结果会缓存，文件没变就不再重新计算；QQ 通知监听不再每秒重建一次系统通知服务；开机自启的注册表读写不再启动 `reg.exe`，打开设置窗口不再为此等待。
- **更新后设置不再被改回去**：之前更新后「贴边样式」可能被改回细线、AI 会话排序被改回「最近」，现在你选过的设置会原样保留。
- **按键更好点**：折叠的小岛两端（最左边的 Agent 图标、最右边的最小化按钮）偶尔点不中的问题已修复，边缘多留了几像素的点击余量。
- **玻璃材质更顺**：液态玻璃、磨砂玻璃展开时下方不再留空白；展开状态切换页面时，底部阴影不再与岛身断开；展开、收起过程中不再先出现边框，动画更流畅。
- **折叠的微信聊天遵守免打扰**：在微信里折叠起来的聊天不会再绕过免打扰弹到小岛上。
- **设置重新整理**：「通用」只放行为相关的设置，「外观」按岛屿的部位（主题与颜色、收起时、展开后、屏幕顶端）重新分组，贴边样式也移到了外观里；所有设置项和已保存的选择保持不变。
- **问题排查日志**：「设置 → 关于」可以打开问题排查日志（默认关闭），遇到问题时方便导出给我们定位；日志最多约 10 MB，反复开关不会让文件变大。
- **测试版通道**：「设置 → 关于」可以主动下载测试版，也可以随时还原到正式版。正式版用户不会自动收到测试版。
- **消息标签可调整顺序**：消息页顶部的微信、QQ、邮箱标签可以长按拖动调整顺序。
- **剪贴板标签更整齐**：文字、图片、文件筛选并入标签行，下划线跟随当前标签。
- **交流群红点点一次就消失**：设置里「交流群」的红点看过一次后不再出现。

## What's new

- **No more stutter while NetEase Cloud Music plays (#93)**: with its progress plugin, NetEase hands Windows the full-size album art, often several MB, and the island used to read and convert it every second, keeping NetEase and the system media service busy. Covers are now scaled down to the size the island shows, a missing cover is retried less and less often, and media events are throttled.
- **Lighter at startup**: the NetEase version check reads only the file header instead of the whole program, the plugin's checksum is cached until the file changes, the QQ notification listener no longer rebuilds the system notification service every second, and launch-at-startup reads and writes the registry directly instead of starting `reg.exe`, so the settings window no longer waits on it.
- **Updates keep your settings**: an update could reset the tuck style to Line and the AI session sort to Recent. Your choices are now kept as they are.
- **Easier clicks**: the buttons at both ends of the collapsed island (the Agent icon on the left, minimize on the right) sometimes missed clicks; the edges now take a few pixels of slack.
- **Smoother glass**: Liquid and Frosted glass no longer leave an empty strip below the expanded island, the bottom shadow stays attached when switching pages while expanded, and the rim no longer appears first while expanding or collapsing.
- **Folded WeChat chats respect Do Not Disturb**: chats folded in WeChat no longer get past Do Not Disturb onto the island.
- **Reorganized settings**: General now holds behaviour only, and Appearance is grouped by part of the island (theme and colours, collapsed, expanded, top of the screen), with the tuck style moved there too. All settings and saved choices are unchanged.
- **Diagnostic log**: turn it on in Settings → About (off by default) to export a log when something goes wrong. It stays under about 10 MB, and switching it on and off does not grow the file.
- **Beta channel**: Settings → About can download a beta on request and restore the stable release at any time. Stable users never get a beta automatically.
- **Reorder message tabs**: long-press and drag the WeChat, QQ and mail tabs at the top of the Messages page.
- **Tidier clipboard tabs**: the Text, Images and Files filters join the tab row, with the underline following the current tab.
- **Community dot clears once seen**: the red dot on Community in settings goes away for good after one look.
