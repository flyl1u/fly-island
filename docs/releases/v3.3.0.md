## What's new

- **Calendar Marks**: Install from Settings → Plugins to see lunar dates, solar terms, holidays and make-up workdays in the Chinese Todo calendar. The monthly grid has more room and clearer date, selection and task markers. By [@gyl-coding](https://github.com/gyl-coding).
- **Plugin market**: MiniMax Code, Device Battery & Audio, and Eye Care are listed as In development and cannot be installed yet.
- **Todo timers**: Run countdowns on multiple tasks at once, with separate controls and reminders.
- **Clipboard**: Hover over a history entry to preview its full contents.
- **Appearance**: The font picker now includes a bundled pixel font and fonts installed in Windows.
- **Startup and media**: Fixed a startup issue that could leave the island layout stuck, and reduced repeated audio initialization in media controls.

## Downloads

- `fly-island_3.3.0_x64-setup.exe`: Windows x64 EXE installer.
- `fly-island_3.3.0_x64_en-US.msi`: Windows x64 MSI installer.
- `.sig`, `latest.json`, and `SHA256SUMS.txt` provide installer signatures, the in-app update manifest, and checksums.
