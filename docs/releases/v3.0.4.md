## 更新内容

- **待办历史**：按天查看以前的待办，标题写「今天 / 昨天 / 11月9日 周一」，旁边显示完成进度；没完成的可以一键移到今天，更早的日子折叠收起。
- **日历**：月视图标出有待办的日子（未完成用强调色、全部完成用灰色），点某天查看当天待办，今天和以后的日子可以直接添加待办。
- **待办页切换**：待办、笔记、历史、日历四个视图用左上角一个分段控件切换，随时一步回到待办。
- **翻译页动画**：在岛上打开快捷翻译时，和其他页面一样顺滑展开。
- **自动收起设置**：「展开后自动收起」和「收起后贴边成细线」分成两项，都可以填写任意秒数，说明更清楚。

待办、笔记、归档、剪贴板收藏和已有设置继续保留。

## What's new

- **Todo history**: browse past todos by day ("Today / Yesterday / Mon, Nov 9") with a completion summary; move unfinished ones to today in one click; older days fold away.
- **Calendar**: a month view marks days with todos (accent for unfinished, grey when all done); pick a day to see its todos, and add todos to today or later days right there.
- **Todo views**: Todos, Notes, History and Calendar switch with one segmented control at the top left, so Todos is always one click away.
- **Translation motion**: the on-island translation page opens with the same smooth spring as the other pages.
- **Auto-collapse settings**: "collapse the open island" and "tuck the collapsed island into a line" are now two separate settings, each taking any number of seconds, with clearer descriptions.

Your todos, notes, archives, clipboard favorites and existing settings are preserved.
