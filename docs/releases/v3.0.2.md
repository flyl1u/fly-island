## 更新内容

- **快捷键**：修复录入的新快捷键无法保存的问题。
- **翻译**：自动识别方向改为按主要语言判断，长段英文里夹几个中文字时也能正确翻成中文；打开翻译界面时直接展开，不再有向下冲的动作。
- **音乐**：进度条和波形改为中性色；音乐设置页提示：若不显示封面，请在对应播放器中开启 SMTC。
- **微信提醒**：点击消息页中间的微信图标即可打开微信，去掉了右上角的跳转箭头。
- **设置**：「关于」移到「连接」正下方，关于页新增「打赏作者」。

待办、笔记、归档、剪贴板收藏和已有设置继续保留。

## What's new

- **Shortcuts**: newly recorded shortcuts are saved again.
- **Translation**: the automatic direction now follows the dominant language, so long English text with a few Chinese characters is translated into Chinese; the translation window opens in place without the drop-down motion.
- **Music**: neutral progress bar and wave; the Music settings page explains that players must enable SMTC for covers to show.
- **WeChat reminders**: click the WeChat icon in the middle of the Messages page to open WeChat; the arrow in the top-right corner is gone.
- **Settings**: About now sits right below Connection, and the About page adds a way to support the author.

Your todos, notes, archives, clipboard favorites and existing settings are preserved.
