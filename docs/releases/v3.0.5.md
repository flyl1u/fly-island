## 更新内容

- **待办历史**按月显示有记录的日期和待办数量。选中一天后，右侧显示当天的清单和完成情况。可以从月份下拉菜单直接跳到往年的记录。
- **待办日历**把月历和当天清单并排放置。即使遇到六周的月份，右侧也有空间显示五条单行待办。

## What's new

- **Todo history** lists dates and task counts by month. Pick a date to see that day's list and completion summary on the right. The month picker can jump directly to records from previous years.
- **Todo calendar** puts the month and the selected day's tasks side by side. Even with a six-week month, the task list has room for five single-line tasks.

## Downloads

- `fly-island_3.0.5_x64-setup.exe` for the Windows x64 EXE installer.
- `fly-island_3.0.5_x64_en-US.msi` for the Windows x64 MSI installer.
- `.sig`, `latest.json` and `SHA256SUMS.txt` provide installer signatures, the in-app update manifest and checksums.
