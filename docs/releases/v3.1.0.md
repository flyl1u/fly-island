## 更新内容

- **剪贴板管理**：历史最多可保存 500 条，岛上一次显示几条也能自己调（3–8 条）。点一次垃圾桶进入多选，每条前面出现勾选框，可以全选，再点一次删除选中的记录。鼠标移到图片缩略图上会变成放大镜，点一下看大图，多选时也能看。
- **剪贴板独立窗口**：设置 →「剪贴板与文件」打开「在单独窗口中打开剪贴板」后，剪贴板会像设置一样开成单独的窗口，可以拖动、调整大小。左上角的图钉让窗口保持在最前面，默认就是钉住的。
- **细线贴边紧贴屏幕上沿**：细线现在贴着屏幕最上沿，唤出时直接从顶边往下长成小岛，不再先往上移一下。贴边样式默认改为细线，升级后会自动换成细线一次，之后想改回「露边」或「隐藏」不会再被改掉。
- **这些时候不贴边**：设置 →「通用」新增三个开关，默认都关闭：Agent 正在运行、小岛在显示歌词、待办已经开始计时。打开后，小岛在这些时候不会自动贴到屏幕顶部，结束后再按设定的时间贴边。
- **贴边圆角**：设置 →「外观 → 外形」可以打开贴边圆角，小岛贴在屏幕顶部时两侧会有向内弯的圆角过渡，圆角大小可调。默认关闭。
- **调设置时直接看到效果**：在设置里切换贴边样式，小岛会马上贴边给你看，约 2.5 秒后自动恢复；调外观和贴边圆角时，小岛也会马上换成对应的样子。
- **消息一键清空**：微信和 QQ 通知的工具栏里，电源按钮左边多了一个按钮，一点就全部已读并清空；邮箱里同一位置的按钮会把邮件全部标为已读。
- **新版本小红点**：有新版本时，岛上「待办」入口会出现一个小红点，待办里的「更新」按钮上也有一个，提醒你去更新。
- **收起时的歌词更准**：展开岛一段时间再收起，小岛上的歌词会直接跳到正在唱的那句，不再停在旧的一句或退回显示歌名；展开期间换了歌，也会提前准备好歌词。

## What's new

- **Clipboard manager**: history now keeps up to 500 entries, and you can choose how many rows the island shows at once (3–8). Click the trash icon once to start selecting: each entry gets a checkbox and you can select all; click it again to delete the selected entries. Hovering an image thumbnail shows a magnifier; click it to see the full image, even while selecting.
- **Clipboard in its own window**: turn on Open in a separate window under Settings → Clipboard & files and the clipboard opens in its own window, like Settings, which you can move and resize. The pin in the top-left corner keeps it on top and is on by default.
- **Line tuck sits flush with the top edge**: the line now touches the very top of the screen and grows straight down into the island, without moving up first. Line is now the default tucked look; after updating you are switched to Line once, and if you change back to Edge or Hidden it stays that way.
- **Stay visible while**: three new switches under Settings → General, all off by default: An agent is working, Lyrics are showing and A to-do is in progress. When on, the island does not tuck to the top of the screen while that is happening and tucks after the usual delay once it ends.
- **Top edge curves**: turn on Top edge curves under Settings → Appearance → Shape to give the island inward curves on both sides where it meets the top of the screen, with an adjustable size. Off by default.
- **See settings as you change them**: switching the tucked look tucks the island right away so you can see it, and it comes back after about 2.5 seconds; appearance and top edge curve changes also show on the island immediately.
- **Clear messages in one click**: the WeChat and QQ toolbars have a new button left of the power button that marks everything read and clears the list; the same button in Mail marks every email as read.
- **New version dot**: when an update is available, a small red dot appears on the island's Todos button and on the Update button inside Todos.
- **More accurate lyrics when collapsed**: after the island has been expanded for a while, the collapsed lyrics jump straight to the current line instead of staying on an old one or falling back to the song title, and lyrics for a song that changed while expanded are fetched in advance.
