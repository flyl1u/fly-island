## 更新内容

- **Codex / GPT 会话状态更准确**：纯思考、工具执行和桌面版会话都能显示运行中；短任务在两次检查之间结束时，也能收到完成提醒。修复部分快速结束或恢复的会话漏提醒，减少后台任务造成的重复提醒。
- **Codex 会话跳转更稳**：改善桌面版会话识别与点击跳转，无法直达具体对话时保留窗口聚焦兜底。
- **待办多任务轮换**：多个已开始的待办各自计时，可在收起的小岛上轮换显示，选择逐条切换或连续滚动。「设置 → 待办 → 轮换显示」默认关闭，按需开启。
- **通知停留时长可调**：「设置 → 消息 → 岛上通知」可设置 3–30 秒的停留时长，默认 5 秒，统一控制 QQ、微信和邮箱的新消息预览。
- **待办日历不再收起小岛**：日期选择改用岛内月历，切换月份和选择日期时保持小岛展开。
- **微信点击定位会话修复**：修复窗口遮挡和等待时序导致的定位失败，点击消息摘要进入对应会话更可靠。
- **更多音乐歌词来源**：增加 QQ 音乐、酷狗本地缓存和汽水音乐歌词支持，改善网易云逐字歌词获取。
- **逐字歌词填充**：支持收起和展开状态的逐字进度填充，优先查找逐字歌词。「设置 → 音乐」中的逐字填充默认关闭，按需开启。

## What's new

- **More accurate Codex / GPT session status**: thinking-only turns, tool execution and desktop sessions now show as running. Tasks completed between checks also trigger completion alerts. Fixes missed alerts for some short or resumed turns and reduces duplicate alerts from background tasks.
- **More reliable Codex session navigation**: improves desktop session detection and click-through, with window focus as a fallback when a specific conversation cannot be opened directly.
- **Rotate active todos**: multiple started todos keep their own timers and can rotate on the collapsed island, either switching one at a time or scrolling continuously. Settings → Todos → Rotate todos is off by default.
- **Adjust notification duration**: Settings → Messages → Island notifications offers 3–30 seconds, with a 5-second default, for new QQ, WeChat and email previews.
- **Todo calendar keeps the island open**: an in-island calendar lets you switch months and choose dates without collapsing the island.
- **WeChat conversation navigation fixes**: fixes timing and overlapping-window issues that could prevent a message preview from opening its conversation.
- **More lyric sources**: adds QQ Music, Kugou's local cache and Qishui Music, and improves NetEase word-timed lyric retrieval.
- **Word-by-word lyric fill**: follows individual words in both the collapsed and expanded island and prefers word-timed lyrics when available. Word fill in Settings → Music is off by default.
