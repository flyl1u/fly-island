## 更新内容

- 启动后自动检查新版本并在后台下载，下载完成后等待你点击更新才安装、重启。
- 检测到新版本时，在今日待办中显示「检测到新版本」，右侧按钮显示下载进度，下载完成后可点击「更新」。
- 更新待办支持改名、勾选、排序和删除；删除相当于稍后处理，设置页仍保留更新入口。
- 更新待办不会写入本地待办存储、每日 Markdown 或归档，也不参与任务轮播。

感谢 [@yiumanlenong](https://github.com/yiumanlenong) 的贡献：[PR #32](https://github.com/flyl1u/fly-island/pull/32)。

## What's new

- The island checks for updates at startup and downloads them in the background. Installation and restart wait for your click.
- A "New version available" task appears in today's list, showing download progress and an Update button when ready.
- You can rename, check off, reorder or delete the update task. Deleting it defers the update; Settings keeps the update action available.
- The update task is excluded from saved tasks, daily Markdown, archives and task rotation.

Thanks to [@yiumanlenong](https://github.com/yiumanlenong) for [PR #32](https://github.com/flyl1u/fly-island/pull/32).
