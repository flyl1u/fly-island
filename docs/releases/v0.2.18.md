## 更新内容

- 暂时关闭液态玻璃风格选项，设置中显示为不可选，并提供「暂不可用」提示。
- 此版本统一使用经典外观，原来使用玻璃风格的用户和样式预设也会按经典外观显示。
- 保留已保存的玻璃风格偏好、强度、颜色、字体与缩放，方便后续恢复；更新不会清空这些设置。
- 保留 v0.2.17 的独立设置窗口、灰色设置小点、对称胶囊形与更新状态同步改进。

## What's new

- Liquid glass is temporarily disabled in Settings, with an unavailable tooltip.
- This version uses the classic appearance, including when a saved preference or preset selects liquid glass.
- Saved glass preferences, intensity, colors, fonts and scale remain intact for a future restoration.
- The independent Settings window, gray settings dot, symmetric capsule and update synchronization improvements from v0.2.17 remain available.
