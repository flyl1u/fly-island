## What's new

- **Dark todos**: todos, daily notes and scheduled tasks now share the island's dark look. Removed the paper background, ruled lines, creases and the Today / Save / History buttons on the left. Automatic saving continues.
- **Clipboard history**: replaced nested cards and boxed icons with a simple list and subtle separators. Search now sits to the right of All / Starred, making the page more compact. Search, favorites, notes and copying remain available.
- **Island appearance**: smaller corners when collapsed, short page capsules that grow on hover, more compact todo spacing, and smoother expansion and collapse.
- **Appearance settings**: a continuous expanded-size slider with a live preview, plus a position lock to prevent accidental dragging.
- **File shelf**: dragging files out keeps their references on the island by default, so they can be used again.

Your todos, notes, archives, clipboard favorites and existing settings are preserved.
